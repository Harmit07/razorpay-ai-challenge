Codified Regulatory Frameworks

Statutory citations and deterministic rules enforced across every state transition.

100% Invariants Verified
RBI Framework RBI/DPSS/2026-27/396

Mandatory 24h Pre-Debit Notice

Every automated recurring debit retry strictly requires a customer notification delivered at least 24 hours in advance via WhatsApp/SMS containing an instant opt-out link.

AFA Limit Cap RBI AFA 2026

Statutory ₹15k / ₹1L Ceilings

Recurring payments > ₹15,000 prohibit direct auto-debits and automatically convert to dynamic AFA OTP payment links. Relaxed to ₹1,00,000 for Mutual Funds, Insurance, and Credit Cards.

TRAI TCCCPR 08:00–20:00 IST

Customer Quiet Hours & DND

All outbound customer touches generated outside 08:00–20:00 IST are queued until next morning. Promotional messages to National DND registry numbers are strictly suppressed.

CPA 2019 Dispute Quarantine

Active Dispute & Chargeback Freeze

When a transaction is flagged with an active chargeback or customer fraud dispute, all automated retries and dunning communications instantly freeze (STOP_DISPUTE_FRAUD).

DPDP Act 2023 PII Protection

Customer Data Privacy & Masking

All customer identifiers (phone numbers, email addresses) are permanently masked (e.g. +91-9876****4321) across all runtime audit trails, logs, and database records.

MSMED Act 2006 Section 15 & 16

B2B 45-Day Overdue Ceiling

Commercial invoices approaching the statutory 45-day overdue boundary are automatically clamped and escalated directly to finance operations rather than standard customer nudges.
