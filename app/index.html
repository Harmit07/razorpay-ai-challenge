Payment & E-Mandate Controls (RBI Framework)
Mandatory 24h Pre-Debit Notice Delayed (24h)
TRIGGER An automated recurring debit retry is scheduled on an e-mandate instrument.
ACTION Deliver advance notice ≥ 24h in advance via WhatsApp/SMS with instant opt-out link.
STATUTE RBI Master Direction on E-Mandates (RBI/DPSS/2026-27/396) mandates informed customer consent.
Statutory AFA Limit Caps (₹15k / ₹1L) Channel Switch
TRIGGER Transaction amount exceeds ₹15,000 (or ₹1,00,000 for Mutual Funds & Insurance).
ACTION Prohibit direct auto-debit; generate dynamic 1-click AFA OTP checkout link.
STATUTE Additional Factor of Authentication (AFA) is statutory for high-value recurring debits.
Zombie Retry Ceiling (3x Cap) Blocked
TRIGGER Mandate has recorded 3 prior failed recovery touches without settlement.
ACTION Permanently halt automated dunning touches (STOP_MAX_RETRIES).
STATUTE RBI Fair Practices Code prohibits continuous nuisance attempts on unresponsive mandates.
Customer Communication & Timing (TRAI & CPA Framework)
Customer Quiet Hours & DND Delayed (08:30 AM)
TRIGGER Outreach trigger occurs outside 08:00–20:00 IST or destination is registered on DND.
ACTION Hold outreach in compliant queue for 08:30 AM release; suppress promotional dunning on DND.
STATUTE TRAI Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations prohibit off-hour nuisance.
Active Dispute & Chargeback Freeze Blocked
TRIGGER Customer files a fraud dispute or issuing bank records an active chargeback.
ACTION Instantly quarantine transaction and permanently halt all retry touches (STOP_DISPUTE_FRAUD).
STATUTE Consumer Protection Act (CPA 2019) prohibits dunning on actively contested debt.
Promise-to-Pay (PTP) Grace Window Paused
TRIGGER Customer states an explicit commitment date/time via voice or chat transcript.
ACTION Transition FSM state to PTP_FROZEN and suppress outreach until promised settlement date.
STATUTE CPA 2019 prohibits customer harassment while active commitment is in effect.
Data Privacy & Commercial B2B Controls (DPDP & MSMED Framework)
Customer PII Data Masking Enforced
TRIGGER Customer phone numbers or email addresses are ingested or audited.
ACTION Permanently mask identifiers (+91-9876****4321) across all runtime audit logs and UI views.
STATUTE Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023) mandates data minimization and privacy by design.
B2B Overdue Ceiling (45 Days) Escalated
TRIGGER Commercial B2B invoice approaches or exceeds the statutory 45-day overdue boundary.
ACTION Halt customer automated nudges and escalate to finance ops with statutory 3x repo interest (19.5% p.a.).
STATUTE MSMED Act Section 15 & 16 mandates statutory interest and formal dispute escalation.
